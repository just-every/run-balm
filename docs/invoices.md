Balm invoices the client for a visit. Payment happens outside Balm: you record what you receive.

## Prepare and approve

1. On the **Schedule**, mark the visit **attended**.
2. In the client's file, under **Appointments**, select **Prepare invoice**. Leave **Amount in cents** blank for the service's price, or enter another amount (`9500` is $95.00). An assistant with an agent key can prepare it too.
3. Reception or the owner opens the draft, under **Waiting for approval** on **Invoices and reminders** or in the client's file, ticks **I reviewed this exact invoice.** and selects **Approve and number invoice**.

Approval gives the invoice the next number, such as `INV-000014`, and fixes its file for good. If anything the draft froze has changed since (the client, practitioner, service, time or amount), approval is refused: prepare it again.

## Download

**Download invoice** returns the same file every time: the clinic's name, tax ID and address, the client, the practitioner with their profession and provider number, the date of service, the service and the amount. Each download is recorded in the client's access audit.

## Record a payment

Open **Record a payment received**, enter the **Amount received in cents** and a **Payment reference** that is unique, such as `Check 1042`, and select **Record payment**. A reference can be used once, and payments can never add up to more than the invoice. The invoice shows what is still outstanding, or **Paid**.

## Void

The owner can void an approved invoice that has no payments, with a reason. Its number stays used.
