Balm invoices the client for a visit. Patients pay by their card on file, from their private pay link (see [Patient payments](/docs/payments)) or at the desk, where you record what you receive.

## Prepare and approve

1. On the **Schedule**, mark the visit **attended**.
2. In the client's file, under **Appointments**, select **Prepare invoice**. Leave **Amount ($)** blank for the service's price, or enter another amount in dollars, such as `80.00`. An assistant with an agent key can prepare it too.
3. Reception or the owner opens the draft, under **Waiting for approval** on **Invoices and reminders** or in the client's file, ticks **I reviewed this exact invoice.** and selects **Approve and number invoice**.

Approval gives the invoice the next number, such as `INV-000014`, and fixes its file for good. If anything the draft froze has changed since (the client, practitioner, service, time or amount), approval is refused: prepare it again.

## Download

**Download invoice** returns the same file every time: the clinic's name, tax ID and address, the client, the practitioner with their profession and provider number, the date of service, the service and the amount. When the visit has a signed treatment note with CPT codes, the invoice also lists each code with its minutes and its units by the 8-minute rule ([Treatment notes](/docs/notes)). Sign the note before you prepare the invoice: only signed notes carry their units. Each download is recorded in the client's access audit.

## Record a payment

Open **Record a payment received**, enter the **Amount received ($)**, such as `95.00`, and a **Payment reference** that is unique, such as `Check 1042`, and select **Record payment**. A reference can be used once, and payments can never add up to more than the invoice. The invoice shows what is still outstanding, or **Paid**.

## Void

The owner can void an approved invoice that has no payments, with a reason. Its number stays used.
