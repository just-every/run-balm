## The waitlist

The **Waitlist** keeps clients who want an earlier or a better time. Owners and reception see the whole list; a practitioner sees the clients waiting for them.

## Add someone

On the **Waitlist** page use **Add to the waitlist**, or open the client's file and use **Add to the waitlist** under **Appointments**. Choose:

- the **Practitioner**, or **Any practitioner**;
- the **Service** they are waiting for;
- **From** and **Until**: the dates they could come in (From is today when left blank);
- **Preferred days** and optional **From time** and **Until time**, in the clinic time zone; the entire visit must fit within that same day;
- a **Note for staff** if you like.

The client needs an email address, because offers go by email. One client waits once for the same service and practitioner.

## When a visit is canceled

When a booked visit at least two hours ahead is canceled, Balm queues matching clients in the order they joined. Practitioner (or any practitioner), service, dates, preferred weekdays and the entire visit within preferred times must fit. The first eligible client gets an email with a private link and a 15-minute hold. The hold ends no later than one hour before the visit, and protects the practitioner and room from another booking.

- During their hold, the person who selects **Book this time** is booked into the calendar at once, in the same room, and gets the usual confirmation with their intake link. They leave the waitlist.
- Everyone else queued for that time is emailed that it has been taken, and stays on the waitlist. Their link says the same.
- The minute scheduler advances to the next eligible client after a hold expires or its entry is removed. An expired link cannot accept a later turn. Delivery is attempted once for each turn, with its actual outcome recorded; an uncertain or failed attempt is never automatically resent.
- An offer closes one hour before the visit, or when no eligible clients remain. If the time was booked after a hold expired, it cannot be offered or booked again.
- Existing offers created before ordered holds keep their original first-acceptance terms. Restored offers retain history without reserving a time, reviving a link or restarting messages.

The **Offers** panel on the **Waitlist** page lists each offered time with who was emailed, who accepted and who was told it was taken.

## Text messages

Offers by text message are not available yet: Balm sends them by email until the clinic connects a text-message provider.
