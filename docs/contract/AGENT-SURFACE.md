### Agent surface

**Specialist: Therapy practice coordinator.** It follows the clinic's referrals, scheduled care, documentation work, patient communications and payer and patient balances through to a reconciled week.

Inherits BASE-AGENT-001 to BASE-AGENT-013. The company's policy decides every call.

Balm's delta is its clinical scope, evidence custody and business facts below. These are target jobs, including unbuilt ones; Today names an existing native action, module or route, not an MCP implementation. Native clinical roles, patient consent, payer requirements, exact originals and dated retention remain business constraints. A tool records clinical decisions and signatures that actually occurred; it does not invent clinical judgment or impersonate a signer. The company's own policy decides the outcome for each declared class. No caller flag, confirmation boolean, patient message, imported file or model output changes a class.

**How Balm computes each class.** Balm's server uses typed arguments, live native records, actual visibility and authenticated provider responses, not assertions in record text. Every call carries all applicable classes, even when its primary job has a different class. In the table, a single class is fixed, `a · b` always carries both, and `(+ c)` adds c when its test below is met.

- **standard:** internal reads, drafts, proposals and reversible record edits; facts are affected item IDs and count. Reading a balance or forecasting revenue does not move money. An internal read of a prior outside send does not itself contact its recipient.
- **external:** a provider request, outbound email/SMS/fax, outside webhook, subscription feed or content newly visible in a patient or guardian portal; facts are destination, channel, recipient count and counterparty. Counterparty is known for the clinic's recorded patient, guardian, provider, payer, vendor or connected account, new for another identified recipient, and public for open-link exposure. Scheduled-visit notifications count even when generated by the native action. For inference, a clinic-local model adds no external class; a remote model does. For refunds, recording completed outside cash adds no external class; sending Stripe a refund does.
- **money:** invoice issuance or voiding, payment, refund, adjustment, installment obligation, retail sale/redemption/settlement or a changed fee; facts are integer USD cents, currency, clinic account and payee/payer. Changing only a service's duration is not money; changing its price is. Estimates, eligibility benefits, reports, journal copies and reconciliation drafts do not move money or change a charge.
- **commitment:** an issued fixed original, submitted claim, accepted finite obligation, relied-on attestation, discharge/closure or recorded sign-off; facts are exact digest, source revisions, actual actor, counterparty and stated value where present. An unsigned proposal or nonbinding estimate is not a commitment.
- **gated:** the authenticated outside service requires a hosted consent, card-entry, identity/business verification or personal terms step for this call; facts are provider and exact step. A successful provider path has no gated class; a provider response directing the patient to its hosted card step adds it. The continuation describes that provider step without offering a password, CAPTCHA or 2FA tool.
- **access:** changes to staff jobs, guardian delegations, clinical grants, sessions, agent/webhook/feed grants or saved payment authority; facts are people/identities, native target, before/after scope and direction widens or narrows. Profile corrections that change sign-in identity carry access. Reading grant metadata alone is standard, not an authority change.
- **security:** protective settings, session controls, credentials, provider connection custody, backup custody or whole-clinic exports; facts are setting, target and credential operation, with secrets omitted from results. Ordinary records do not become security merely because they are private.
- **destructive:** irreversible eligible patient/staff erasure or retention destruction; facts are the exact inventory and item count, retention basis, hold state and restore-journal position. Removing a reversible draft or retiring an alert is not destructive.
- **sensitive:** the call reads, writes, returns, sends or exposes actual patient health context, insurance/ID images, full tax/ID/bank/card/payroll numbers or encrypted archives containing those records; facts are record count and kind. Calendar visits tied to patients and patient ledger detail contain health context. Aggregate de-identified reports and a clinic identity with EIN only add no sensitive class; the same read with patient rows or a full SSN does. Restricted fields contribute no returned records and remain absent.
- **live:** changes the running clinic's booking/availability behavior, published forms/templates/consent versions, outreach execution rules, protective runtime settings, effective code catalog or real-patient mode; facts are service, setting, old/new revision and affected door. Writing an individual visit or note is a record operation rather than a service configuration change.

Balm uses no accounts class: these jobs connect existing provider accounts and do not create an outside identity, account or paid resource. Provisioning a new outside account is outside this registry.
