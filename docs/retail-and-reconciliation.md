# Cash sales and finance preparations

These commands use the native signed-in owner door, `POST /api/balm/command`, with a JSON object containing `name` and `input`. They are partial capabilities; no provider charge, automatic renewal, refund or outbound request runs here. The existing patient portal supplies the original cash-sale receipts and current statement. Visit superbills retain their existing coding requirements; goods and gift value are not represented as insured services.

`pay.retail.sell` accepts `clientId`, `kind` (`package`, `membership`, `product` or `gift_card`), `label`, positive integer `amountCents`, a unique `reference` for cash actually received, and `confirm: true`. Packages and finite memberships also require `serviceId` and `units`. Product sales require `taxState`, `taxEvidence` and an owner-verified `taxBasisPoints` rate, with tax rounded once to the nearest cent. Balm does not decide tax applicability. Pass `giftId` instead of recording new cash to redeem that patient's gift value. Gift purchases cannot use gift value. No expiry or transfer is implemented.

Before attendance, `pay.retail.assign` takes `clientId`, `saleId` and `appointmentId` for the matching cash-pay service. Attendance atomically consumes one visit, refuses exhaustion, and starts a zero-dollar invoice draft. Invoice preparation cannot increase that covered visit's charge. Cancelled and no-show visits do not consume package visits. The staff patient JSON includes `billing.retail` with remaining visits and gift value. Sale and gift totals appear in financial reports; redemption posts existing gift value without another deposit. Patient gift value remains separate from general invoice credit.

`finance.observation.record` takes `clientId`, `kind` (`eft` or `card`), positive `amountCents`, `occurredOn`, unique settlement `reference`, `sourceReference` (the original EFT trace or card charge), and `evidence` identifying the owner-reviewed source. These are observations, never an adjudication or proof that a claim is paid. No bank credentials or account numbers are needed. Repeated card settlement references for the same patient, source charge and amount are flagged for review, not declared fraudulent or automatically refunded.

Create an agent key explicitly scoped to `finance.reconcile.prepare`. Through `POST /api/balm/agent/command`, that command takes `month` in YYYY-MM form and creates cited, immutable request and refund drafts. It does not send a request. The owner uses `finance.review.approve` with `id`, the returned `sha256`, and `confirm: true` to approve the exact preparation once. That approval neither issues a refund nor sends a message; execution and full provider reconciliation remain unbuilt. Legacy keys gain no finance scope.

Both capabilities use conservative defaults pending ct review. Retention destruction and ordinary patient erasure refuse these new financial records until custody review and anonymous accounting preservation are implemented. Archives carry the records and frozen originals; keys and sessions remain excluded under the existing archive policy. No complete retention or reconciliation claim is made.
