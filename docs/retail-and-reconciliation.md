These commands use the native signed-in owner door, `POST /api/balm/command`, with a JSON object containing `name` and `input`. They are partial capabilities; no provider charge, automatic renewal, refund or outbound request runs here. The existing patient portal supplies the original cash-sale receipts and current statement. Visit superbills retain their existing coding requirements; goods and gift value are not represented as insured services.

`pay.retail.sell` accepts `clientId`, `kind` (`package`, `membership`, `product` or `gift_card`), `label`, positive integer `amountCents`, a unique `reference` for cash actually received, and `confirm: true`. Packages and finite memberships also require `serviceId` and `units`. Product sales require `taxState`, `taxEvidence` and an owner-verified `taxBasisPoints` rate, with tax rounded once to the nearest cent. Balm does not decide tax applicability. Pass `giftId` instead of recording new cash to redeem that patient's gift value. Gift purchases cannot use gift value. No expiry or transfer is implemented. The owner records all four sale kinds in the patient file under Cash sales and visit packages; cash-pay visit assignment is available there too. Reservations cannot exceed unused visits.

Before attendance, `pay.retail.assign` takes `clientId`, `saleId` and `appointmentId` for the matching cash-pay service. Attendance atomically consumes one visit, refuses exhaustion, and starts a zero-dollar invoice draft. Invoice preparation cannot increase that covered visit's charge. Cancelled and no-show visits do not consume package visits. The staff patient JSON includes `billing.retail` with remaining visits and gift value. Sale and gift totals appear in financial reports; redemption posts existing gift value without another deposit. Patient gift value remains separate from general invoice credit.

`finance.observation.record` takes `clientId`, `kind` (`eft` or `card`), positive `amountCents`, `occurredOn`, unique settlement `reference`, `sourceReference` (the original EFT trace or card charge), and `evidence` identifying the owner-reviewed source. These are observations, never an adjudication or proof that a claim is paid. No bank credentials or account numbers are needed. Repeated card settlement references for the same patient, source charge and amount are flagged for review, not declared fraudulent or automatically refunded.

Create an agent key explicitly scoped to `finance.reconcile.prepare`. Through `POST /api/balm/agent/command`, that command takes `month` in YYYY-MM form and creates cited, immutable request and refund drafts. It does not send a request. The owner uses `finance.review.approve` with `id`, the returned `sha256`, and `confirm: true` to approve the exact preparation once. That approval neither issues a refund nor sends a message; execution and full provider reconciliation remain unbuilt. Legacy keys gain no finance scope.

Ct approved the limited conservative defaults with changes on 25 October 2026; the adopted-default riders in CONTRACT.md and ACCEPTANCE.md bind future execution and custody. Ordinary patient erasure refuses these financial originals. Approved retention destruction now removes retail identifying records, receipt bytes, assignments, uses and membership events after the applicable retained period, measured from the later treatment, sale, redemption, actual visit consumption or membership event. Unused visits and gift value block destruction; completing their actual use resolves that fence without forfeiture. It preserves anonymous dated sale amounts, tax, units and gift arithmetic for financial reports. Legal holds and digest review remain mandatory. Finance records still refuse destruction pending their complete custody implementation. Archives carry the records and frozen originals; keys and sessions remain excluded under the existing archive policy. No complete retention or reconciliation claim is made.


`pay.retail.membership` accepts `clientId`, `saleId`, `state` (`paused` or `active`), `reason`, `consentEvidence`, `effectiveAt`, `authorizedAt`, `authorizationMethod` and `confirm`. Effective and authorization times are UTC ISO timestamps, authorization must precede the effective change, and future-effective changes refuse. The evidence names the specific patient-authorized transition; general intake, treatment or card consent is insufficient. The event retains the unused count, actor and both times. Historical events without these fields remain historical evidence with unknown authorization details; restore never supplies missing facts. Only the active owner records an explicit patient-authorized change. A paused membership cannot be assigned or consumed, including a visit assigned before the pause. Resume preserves every unused visit. Repeated identical changes refuse; concurrent changes require review. Exhaustion prevents further assignment. A later cash purchase is a separate sale with a separate original; it is never an automatic renewal or a charge. Ct approved the reversible pause default with riders on 26 October 2026. PAY-008 remains partial.

`pay.retail.cancel` uses the same explicit transition authorization fields and accepts only an exhausted membership. It records cancellation once, keeps the original receipt and pause history, and blocks subsequent resume or assignment. Cancellation with unused visits refuses, preserving the entitlement. Recording cancellation neither changes an external billing arrangement nor initiates any refund, charge or renewal. CT RL-104 on 28 October 2026 approves this owner-recorded, specifically patient-authorized exhausted cancellation default with riders. Settlement of unused entitlements, complete cancellation and renewal, staff sale authority, provider handoff, patient-facing notices and complete membership reporting remain Gaps.

`finance.source.record` accepts the same patient, original reference, source evidence and transaction date as a settlement observation, with `kind` of `charge`, `payout` or `accounting`, positive integer `amountCents`, a unique `reference` and `confirm: true`. Only payouts accept nonnegative `feeCents`. A payout source is the owner's explicitly allocated patient-and-charge portion, not an imported whole processor payout. No provider or accounting account is connected.

The monthly preparation returns `matching`, with one entry for every recorded settlement and source. Each names its amount, original reference, cited evidence, group totals and every missing or differing source. Payout net plus fees is compared with settlements; charges and accounting amounts are compared separately. Patient identity and requested transaction month bound each group; equal references on another patient never join. Adding the missing accounting remainder can make the recorded amounts agree, but this status grants no provider verification, payment posting or execution authority. Request and refund drafts retain their previous preparation-only boundaries. Complete payout allocation, cross-period reconciliation, actual ERA pursuit and finance-record destruction remain unproved.


`pay.retail.renew` records an explicitly patient-authorized new finite cash membership only after the source membership is exhausted and not cancelled. It requires `clientId`, `saleId`, `kind: membership`, the same `serviceId`, new `label`, `units`, `amountCents` and `reference`, every transition authorization field above, and `cashConfirm: true` for cash already received. The new immutable receipt names its source; the old receipt and history do not change. A source may be renewed once. Non-owner, wrong-patient, cancelled, unconsumed, gift-funded and representative-authorized requests refuse. CT RL-104 approves this finite cash-renewal default with riders; no automatic debit or external billing change occurs.

The signed-in patient portal lists recorded membership lifecycle notices and unused visits beside each original purchase receipt. It shows no internal consent evidence and sends no outbound notice. Financial reports add a retail obligations CSV with state counts, sold and used visits, unused visits, purchase price allocated to unused visits, renewal counts and separately held gift value, as of the selected period end. The allocated value is not a refund quote. Approved destruction preserves only anonymous dated arithmetic and state facts; older destroyed purchases without those facts are explicitly reported as incomplete historical obligations. Outbound notices, provider integration, unused-entitlement settlement and broader renewal authority remain unproved.


`pay.retail.settle` records an already completed external cash refund and cancels an unused finite membership. It takes the same patient and membership identifiers and transition authorization fields, plus `settlementAmountCents`, `settlementReference`, `settlementEvidence` and `cashReturned: true`. The amount must equal the original cash purchase price multiplied by unused visits and divided by all purchased visits, rounded once to cents. Cancel any booked assigned visits before settlement. The record atomically cancels and settles once, preserves the original receipt, and leaves actual attendance intact. It rejects gift-funded memberships and representative assertions. Ledger adjustments and the cash refund, deposit reconciliation, dated obligations, portal notice and accounting preparation retain the same amount; archive restore and approved destruction preserve their relevant facts. CT RL-104 approves this recorded-cash default with riders, without certifying completion. Balm initiates no provider refund and does not verify an entered cash refund.


The owner can select **Review eligible retail originals** in the patient’s retention panel. `retention.retail.preview` takes `clientId` and returns a digest-bound inventory of eligible retail groups. `retention.retail.destroy` requires that `previewId`, `digest` and `confirm: true`. This destroys only the reviewed retail originals and transition evidence; the patient, clinical records and other purchases remain. Gift funding and its redemptions stay together, as do a membership and its renewals. The latest event in each connected group extends that group’s applicable retention period. Clinical deadlines, the documented adult and minor rules, payer and practice requirements, legal holds, unused value and booked assignments continue to protect affected records. A newer unrelated retail purchase does not extend an older group’s deadline.

Approval expires after fifteen minutes; any inventory, authority, hold or policy change requires a fresh review. A full-patient preview cannot authorize retail-only destruction, or the reverse. Anonymous dated accounting and covered-visit facts survive; deleting a package receipt never makes its covered visit billable again. Opaque transaction fingerprints prevent replay of retired receipt and cash-settlement references without retaining their private text. Older archives cannot restore the retired originals against the current erasure authority or its later journal. A fresh destination without that journal still has unverified erasure history. Independent clinical destruction while newer retail remains, detailed provider custody and complete membership lifecycle handling remain unfinished.
