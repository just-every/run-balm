On **Clinic → Automatic visit reminders**, enter up to three lead times in minutes (for example `2880, 1440` for 48 and 24 hours). Balm checks booked visits every minute through Cloudflare Cron and sends email without an approval step. Each lead time sends one reminder. A visit booked after one or more lead times have passed gets a single reminder on the next check, never two at once, and the later lead times still send on schedule: with `1440, 60`, a visit booked 3 hours ahead gets one reminder now and one an hour before; a visit booked 55 minutes ahead gets one. Empty lead times turn reminders off.

These automatic reminders are the ones Balm emails. **Invoices and reminders → Prepared reminders** is different: you (or an agent) prepare exact reminder text, a person approves it, and you download it and send it your own way; Balm does not send that text.

Use the clinic’s `MAIL_PROVIDER`, `MAIL_API_KEY` and verified `MAIL_FROM` deployment settings. The deployment declaration registers `* * * * *`; a self-hosted Worker deployment must retain that Cron schedule and the exported scheduled handler. Cloudflare may take time to propagate a new schedule. Local development suppresses mail and keeps no messages. The normal pinned `rer dev` owner entry opens local sign-in.

The patient follows the private visit link from their reminder and chooses **Confirm my visit** or **Ask for a new time**. The schedule and file show the response. A change request also emails the owner a notice and file link. The booked time stays reserved until the clinic moves or cancels it. Moving or canceling invalidates old reply links and pending deliveries; the scheduler reads the current appointment before sending. Links expire when the visit starts.

**Reminder deliveries** on the client file records each attempt, channel, due time and outcome. **Sent** means accepted by the provider, not proof of inbox delivery. A definite refusal shows **failed**. A lost response or interrupted send shows **uncertain**; Balm does not automatically resend an ambiguous attempt and risk duplicate patient messages. Check with the patient and provider before sending another notice.

The same Twilio adapter handles reminder texts and text-to-pay. Texts connect once the clinic’s US A2P registration is approved and `SMS_PROVIDER=twilio`, `TWILIO_ACCOUNT_SID`, `TWILIO_AUTH_TOKEN` and `TWILIO_FROM` are configured on its own Worker. The clinic must enable reminder texts and record each patient’s consent on their file. Provider requests are exercised with the recorded Twilio fixture in the integration tests; this does not claim live SMS delivery. Inbound SMS, STOP/HELP and quiet hours are not implemented yet.

Use **Secure messages** on a patient file to send a message or reply in its conversation. Patients send from their signed-in portal or a private message link. They choose their practitioner for clinical questions or **Front desk** for scheduling and billing. Practitioners see their own conversations; reception sees only front-desk conversations; the owner sees both. Replies keep the same destination.

A patient or staff member can attach one PNG, JPEG, WebP or PDF up to 1 MB. Balm retains the original bytes. Downloads require that patient's portal or scoped private link, or a staff session with access to the destination; every download is audited. A private link opens only its own conversation. Each patient message also receives a stored automatic reply explaining that this inbox is not for emergencies and to call 911 in an emergency.

Messages and attachments stay in Balm. Emails carry only a generic notice and a private link, never message text or an attachment's name. Patient replies notify the selected practitioner, or active reception staff for the front desk. Links expire in seven days; a clinic reply replaces earlier links for that destination, and **Revoke patient links for my conversations** closes the staff member's links immediately. Reads, messages, attachments, automatic replies and link changes are audited without clinical text. Archives retain the records and omit active patient-link credentials.
