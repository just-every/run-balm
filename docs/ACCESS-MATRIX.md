# Staff access

The owner assigns and revokes each job in **Clinic → Staff**. Job changes and deactivation take effect on the next request, including an existing session. A command checks the staff version again in its write transaction. Refusals are audited without submitted clinical text, including an inactive staff session. The owner downloads the latest access refusals from Clinic → Staff.

| Job | Reads | Writes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Owner | All clinic records; every clinical read audited | Existing owner administration and approvals |
| Practitioner | Their calendar, treating-patient files, their own notes; care-team grants and recorded emergency access as before | Their calendar, clinical records and their own signatures |
| Reception | Clinic calendar, administrative files and billing; no note text, clinical intake or chart search | Existing reception work; no owner administration or clinical signatures |
| Biller | Administrative patient files, invoices, signed charge facts, claims and financial reports/CSVs; no note text, intake answers, chart document metadata, operational reports or owner settings | Invoices and recorded payments; exact-digest adjustment/credit/refund preparation and approval; patient payment links/plans/reminders; coverage, authorization, eligibility, claim, batch, acknowledgment, remittance and recognized-expense billing commands. Estimate requests/preparation (owner issues), and preparation of a selected signed-note release for a recorded payer request/appeal. Note release text is available only after exact-PDF owner review/approval, for seven days or until revoked. No clinical attestations, calendar writes, patient identity changes or configuration |
| PTA/COTA/SLPA assistant, student, clinical fellow | Their own calendar and treating-patient files, with existing clinical read audits | Own note drafts, revisions and objective measures (assistants: daily templates only, never evaluation codes); author signature submitted to a named licensed supervisor. An explicit documentation review uses the already configured clinic model and includes the supervision rule. Students/fellows never finalize independently. Assistants finalize daily notes only under an explicit named-author state/current-payer rule with dated source permissions; a named therapist separately attests actual supervision before claiming. No clinical addendum, billing, emergency opening, calendar write or administration |
| Supervisor | Practitioner access plus the exact notes submitted to them and their review history. Submission alone grants no other patient-file access or contact/demographic details. Returning the note revokes that access until the author submits again | Own practitioner work; co-sign or return their named submissions. No editing another author's revision |
| Location manager | Schedule and visit-count reports for the one assigned location; each named patient audited | Book existing location patients, move/cancel visits, and check in without charging, in assigned rooms only. Both source and destination are guarded; no patient-file or other commands |

New commands default to refusal for billers, supervised staff and location managers until explicitly included. Agent keys keep their existing preparation-only permissions and never become staff.

**Locations** group treatment rooms. The owner assigns a room before it is used; a room with visits cannot be moved to another location because that would change historical scope. Visits with an unassigned room or no room are excluded from a location manager's view. Location managers cannot follow a patient link or obtain an unscoped clinic state. Their choices include only patient names previously scheduled at their location, active practitioner/service names and their assigned rooms.

**Supervision** records an immutable author signature on the exact note revision. The revision and measures freeze while awaiting review. A named, active licensed supervisor of the author’s recorded PT, OT or SLP discipline can return it with a reason; that history remains and the author saves a new revision before submitting again. Co-signing records the actual supervision (in the room, on site or general) and locks the note with both signatures and a digest binding them. Existing care-team and emergency readers retain audited access. Each payer’s owner-recorded rule states whether supervised visits are billable, their minimum supervision and rendering clinician; an unrecorded rule refuses claiming. Medicare students require in-room supervision and Medicare assistants/students bill under the therapist. Confirm the rule in **Clinic → Supervised visit billing**. A visit with conflicting rendering supervisors is refused rather than guessed.

Owners filter Operational reports and every CSV by location. Visit/evaluation locations come from their room; assign weekly hours to a location in Clinic for utilization. Plans count once at the source note’s visit location (the latest plan for an episode), with completion for the entire course. Referrals use the first episode visit’s location. Unassigned records remain explicit; anonymous retained business facts keep their opaque location id. SCHED-007 inherits these filters when it adds service availability by location.

**Staff second step.** Every staff job above completes its own authenticator verification before opening patient records. The owner controls the clinic-wide setting, idle timeout and remote session sign-out at **Staff security**. The default idle timeout is 15 minutes; a shared workstation should be signed out when leaving it. Authenticator seeds are encrypted. One-use recovery codes are shown once during verified setup; there is no email-only reset or administrative bypass. Factors, recovery codes and device proofs do not travel in exports, and a restored clinic enrolls fresh factors. SEC-001 remains partial for ct approval of these conservative defaults and federated role-mapping proof.
